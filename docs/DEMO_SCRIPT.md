# Demo script (every line below is a working command)

## 60-second live recording (desktop Chrome, https://boliverse.vercel.app/#/studio)

| t | Action | Say (or click the matching suggestion) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Landing hero, then **Enter the Studio** | – |
| 5–14 s | Press the mic or **Space** | “Ek beautiful Indian village banao, beech mein nadi ho, aur wooden bridge ho.” |
| 14–21 s | Point at the Voice Studio “Heard” panel: transcript, language, Voice→world ms | “Bridge thoda bada karo, aur sunset kar do.” |
| 21–30 s | Watch the debris fall and the cart stop (“Route blocked”) | “Ab bridge tod do.” |
| 30–37 s | Marathi rebuild, route reopens | “त्याच जागी दगडी पूल बांधा.” |
| 37–46 s | Mansion and furnishing | “Build a luxury mansion with a swimming pool.” then “Mansion ke andar sofa, kaleen aur jhoomar lagao.” |
| 46–60 s | Film: the timeline opens, render runs, then **Download MP4** | “Mansion ke andar camera le jao, 12 second ka video banao.” |

## 2-minute backup

Add these between the steps above: “Undo” then “Redo”; “Paint it red”; “मंदिर के पास तीन घर बनाओ”; “Push the rocks”; “raat kar do aur barish karo”; open the Inspector to show transform, material, physics and environment; toggle **Timbre replies** and speak once to hear Gnani Timbre answer.

## 9:16 social cut

In the Film timeline choose **9:16**, pick *Bridge flythrough* or *Interior walkthrough*, then **Record**. It exports a 720×1280 MP4. Keep captions in the middle 80 % of the frame for Reels and Shorts safe zones.
